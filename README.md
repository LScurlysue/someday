# Someday ✦

A bucket list app for dreaming, visualising and manifesting.

- **Explore**: 200+ ready-made dream ideas (cruises, train trips, dolphins, cities to live in, dream home…), tap ♡ to add
- **My list**: your own dreams, with an "Imagine it" prompt, your scene, map/street links, photos and status (Dreaming, Planning, Manifested ✨)
- **Daydream**: one dream at a time with a 60-second visualisation timer
- **Quotes**: public-domain classics, affirmations and your own favourite quotes
- **Share**: send a link, friends pick the dreams they like for their own list

Installable PWA, works offline, data stays on your device.

Live: https://lscurlysue.github.io/someday/
